Decadent Molten Chocolate Lava Cake 🍰

A rich, warm chocolate cake shell with a signature velvety, flowing molten chocolate core.

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 12 mins
Total Time 27 mins
Servings
4

Preparation Steps

Step 1 of 6 16%
15:00
  1. 1

    Prep & Preheat

    Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C). Butter and lightly flour four 6-ounce ramekins, tapping out excess flour to prevent sticking.

  2. 2

    Melt Chocolate & Butter

    Melt the butter and chopped bittersweet chocolate together in a double boiler or in a microwave-safe bowl in 20-second increments, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly.

  3. 3

    Whisk Eggs & Sugar

    In a medium bowl, vigorously whisk the whole eggs, egg yolks, sugar, and salt together until pale, thickened, and slightly voluminous (about 2-3 minutes of rapid whisking).

  4. 4

    Combine & Fold

    Gently fold the cooled melted chocolate mixture and the flour into the egg mixture using a rubber spatula until just combined. Be careful not to overmix to maintain cake texture.

  5. 5

    Pour & Bake

    Divide the batter evenly among the prepared ramekins. Bake for 12-14 minutes until the outer edges are set and firm, but the center still jiggles slightly (indicating a molten core).

  6. 6

    Invert & Serve

    Let cooling in ramekins for exactly 1 minute. Place an inverted dessert plate over the top of each ramekin, carefully invert it, and lift the ramekin. Garnish with vanilla ice cream and raspberry.
